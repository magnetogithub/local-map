# Map data and font attribution

Map geometry and names are derived from Natural Earth Cultural Vectors: Admin 0 Countries 1:50m, Admin 0 Countries 1:10m, Admin 0 Label Points 1:10m, Admin 1 States and Provinces 1:10m, and Populated Places 1:10m. Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The checked-in snapshots originate from the official `natural-earth-vector` repository. `scripts/prepare-map-data.mjs` normalizes identifiers, validates every Admin 1 parent, separates playable states from technical map units, and derives label baselines. Political boundaries follow the source representation; Pax Local adds no independent territorial claims.

MapLibre glyph PBF files use Open Sans Regular from OpenMapTiles fonts. Open Sans and the local Noto Sans CJK KR font are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Noto source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk

The build-time geometry cleanup preserves closed rings and minimum coordinate counts. It does not claim full topological repair or legal boundary accuracy.

Original metadata, citations, license investigation and all 228 actual features (metadata reports 229) are retained in `data/raw/prompt19/KOR/geoboundaries-adm2-2020-9469f095/` and `data/source-locks/prompt19/KOR-ADM2-2020.json`.

North Korean detail uses geoBoundaries gbOpen PRK ADM2, PRK-ADM2-82179303, revision `9469f09`: World Food Programme and OCHA ROAP, distributed under CC BY 3.0 IGO (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/). Original distribution: https://data.humdata.org/dataset/dpr-korea-administrative-boundaries. The gbOpen 2019 and related humanitarian 2013 metadata conflict; the 2023 build date is not an effective or publication date. Records are in `data/source-locks/prompt19/PRK.json`.

Changes: properties normalized; actual source, fixed project country envelopes and coarse parent boundaries jointly noded. Current fine assignments require original interior membership; source holes and unmatched faces are not assigned by boundary contact or inferred continuation. Original inputs remain unchanged. Source-free fixed-envelope land retains explicit coarse provenance (643 KOR and 488 PRK cells), without an ADM2 identity. The previous contact-based arrangement remains historical evidence. These datasets are not a certification of exact boundaries on 2020-01-01.

Current macro regions are unions of complete fine-source units, with boundaries promoted from the same shared edges. Old Natural Earth ADM1 cuts inside a fine unit are removed rather than independently overlaid. KOR fine-to-macro membership uses City Population administrative hierarchy facts, representative-point identity checks for duplicate names, and the documented 2023 Gunwi transfer date for the 2020 scenario; the evidence is pinned in `data/source-locks/prompt19/KOR-macro-hierarchy.json`. PRK uses exact containment in the same WFP/OCHA ADM1 release (CC BY 3.0 IGO), with all eleven parent unions matching their child ADM2 units; see `data/source-locks/prompt19/PRK-macro-hierarchy.json`. Hierarchy grouping does not move the fixed project national borders or assign unverified source-free residuals to named macro regions.

The gameplay partition additionally reconciles narrow country/coast residual strips with adjacent fine families, using positive-length contacts and witnessed continuations of shared fine boundaries. National borders and coasts are edges of that same final partition. This is an explicitly recorded gameplay adjustment, not a claim that the resulting outlines are unmodified or authoritative administrative boundaries. Raw source artifacts and every original fine family's footprint remain preserved. Small source-free land loops may join their sole surrounding fine family when their area is at most 3 km² and all vertices are within 5 km of the national/coast outline. Long thin strips up to 250 km² and effective width 2 km retain the original shared-terminal, same-pair corridor and 10 km continuation requirements. Farther inland holes, broad missing districts, islands without fine-source attribution, actual country holes, and ambiguous border sectors remain preserved. A shared precision grid of 1e-12 degrees prevents independent floating-point intersections from creating sliver faces. Explicit removal checks for eight reported loops and both eastern coastal strips, together with independent geometry checks, are recorded in `reports/maintenance/20261030-boundary-cleanup-independent-verification.json`.

The remaining connected western KOR gameplay gap is completed separately using only actual positive-length contact lines with the existing Paju and Yeoncheon fine families. A bounded local point-site Voronoi partition samples these lines at intervals no greater than 250 m; its distance-estimation bound is recorded and does not claim a positional accuracy bound for an official administrative border. Only the two source-free input faces are assigned. All original fine land, national/coast outlines, country ownership and verified macro ancestry remain preserved. The full Korean shared border is now covered by fine gameplay territory on both sides. Assignment geometry, independent isolation checks and exact replay evidence are pinned in `reports/maintenance/20261030-western-sector-independent-verification.json`.

CHN experimental inputs use geoBoundaries gbOpen CHN ADM3, boundary ID `CHN-ADM3-62558664`, fixed revision `9469f09592ced973a3448cf66b6100b741b64c0d`: © OpenStreetMap contributors / Lee Beryman, Wambacher, distributed by geoBoundaries / William & Mary geoLab. The source database and adapted CHN database are offered under [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/); [OpenStreetMap attribution](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) is required. geoBoundaries distribution attribution also applies; its fixed LICENSE explicitly preserves each input's own license. This does not replace the licenses of the independently retained Natural Earth/KOR/PRK inputs. The unchanged raw source, source archive, normalized CHN database, original license evidence and derivation are available locally in `data/raw/chn-2020/`, `data/source-locks/chn-2020/`, and `scripts/chn-2020/`.

The CHN GeoJSON contains 2,864 actual features, while upstream metadata and its original DBF list 2,867. The three DBF-only records are not fabricated as polygons. Distributor `shapeID` values are retained; original official/OSM identifiers and parent fields are absent. Metadata says represented year 2017, but the source XML records August 2021 processing. These are **mixed/uncertified-vintage experimental inputs for a 2020-01-01 scenario**, not verified exact 2020 administrative boundaries. The 2020-published geoBoundaries 3.0.0 alternative was inspected and rejected because of material internal overlaps and larger fixed-envelope gaps. Official NGCC distribution did not provide a confirmed general redistribution grant. Direct source URLs, adoption/exclusion reasons, sizes and hashes are recorded in `reports/chn-2020/source-decision.json`.

Only land already classified CHN by the active game is eligible. Fine families are clipped to that fixed partition; source polygons outside it do not annex Hong Kong, Macao, Taiwan, another country's land, or sea. Whole-source containment in the compatible OSM-derived historical ADM1 dataset establishes 1,660 macro assignments, with three separately proved floating-point exceptions. The other 1,201 source families retain explicitly unconfirmed macro ancestry using the existing country-envelope-residual contract. They are not assigned by centroid, name similarity or greatest overlap. Macro boundaries are promoted from complete fine families' shared partition edges. Positive-contact gameplay residual corrections have separate geometry and area ledgers and make no administrative-truth claim. No nationwide grid or Voronoi replaces the administrative polygons; contact-sampled Voronoi is restricted to existing source-free residuals, with a documented local distance approximation and 10 km bound. Source-free islands, broad gaps and actual fixed-country holes remain explicit.
