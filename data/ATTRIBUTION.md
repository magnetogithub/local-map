# Map data and font attribution

Map geometry and names are derived from Natural Earth Cultural Vectors: Admin 0 Countries 1:50m, Admin 0 Countries 1:10m, Admin 0 Label Points 1:10m, Admin 1 States and Provinces 1:10m, and Populated Places 1:10m. Natural Earth data is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

The checked-in snapshots originate from the official `natural-earth-vector` repository. `scripts/prepare-map-data.mjs` normalizes identifiers, validates every Admin 1 parent, separates playable states from technical map units, and derives label baselines. Political boundaries follow the source representation; Pax Local adds no independent territorial claims.

MapLibre glyph PBF files use Open Sans Regular from OpenMapTiles fonts. Open Sans and the local Noto Sans CJK KR font are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Noto source: https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk

The build-time geometry cleanup preserves closed rings and minimum coordinate counts. It does not claim full topological repair or legal boundary accuracy.

The dataset credits geoBoundaries and Thomas Brinkhoff / City Population and designates CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/); geoBoundaries distribution is CC BY 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Original metadata, citations, license investigation and all 228 actual features (metadata reports 229) are retained in `data/raw/prompt19/KOR/geoboundaries-adm2-2020-9469f095/` and `data/source-locks/prompt19/KOR-ADM2-2020.json`.

Changes: properties normalized; actual source, fixed project country envelopes and coarse parent boundaries jointly noded. Current fine assignments require original interior membership; source holes and unmatched faces are not assigned by boundary contact or inferred continuation. Original inputs remain unchanged. Source-free fixed-envelope land retains explicit coarse provenance (643 KOR and 488 PRK cells), without an ADM2 identity. The previous contact-based arrangement remains historical evidence. These datasets are not a certification of exact boundaries on 2020-01-01.

Current macro regions are unions of complete fine-source units, with boundaries promoted from the same shared edges. Old Natural Earth ADM1 cuts inside a fine unit are removed rather than independently overlaid. KOR fine-to-macro membership uses City Population administrative hierarchy facts, representative-point identity checks for duplicate names, and the documented 2023 Gunwi transfer date for the 2020 scenario; the evidence is pinned in `data/source-locks/prompt19/KOR-macro-hierarchy.json`. PRK uses exact containment in the same WFP/OCHA ADM1 release (CC BY 3.0 IGO), with all eleven parent unions matching their child ADM2 units; see `data/source-locks/prompt19/PRK-macro-hierarchy.json`. Hierarchy grouping does not move the fixed project national borders or assign unverified source-free residuals to named macro regions.

The gameplay partition additionally reconciles narrow country/coast residual strips with adjacent fine families, using positive-length contacts and witnessed continuations of shared fine boundaries. National borders and coasts are edges of that same final partition. This is an explicitly recorded gameplay adjustment, not a claim that the resulting outlines are unmodified or authoritative administrative boundaries. Raw source artifacts and every original fine family's footprint remain preserved. Small source-free land loops may join their sole surrounding fine family when their area is at most 3 km² and all vertices are within 5 km of the national/coast outline. Long thin strips up to 250 km² and effective width 2 km retain the original shared-terminal, same-pair corridor and 10 km continuation requirements. Farther inland holes, broad missing districts, islands without fine-source attribution, actual country holes, and ambiguous border sectors remain preserved. A shared precision grid of 1e-12 degrees prevents independent floating-point intersections from creating sliver faces. Explicit removal checks for eight reported loops and both eastern coastal strips, together with independent geometry checks, are recorded in `reports/maintenance/20261030-boundary-cleanup-independent-verification.json`.
